# iOS Setup Notes

Add these keys to your Info.plist (Runner/Info.plist):

<key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key>
<string>StockMate needs camera access to scan barcodes and serial numbers.</string>
<string>StockMate, barkod ve seri numaralarini taramak icin kamera erisimi gerektirir.</string>
<key>NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription</key>
<string>StockMate needs access to your photo library.</string>

The app uses HTTP (not HTTPS) to connect to your local office server.
Add to Info.plist under NSAppTransportSecurity:
<key>NSAppTransportSecurity</key>
<dict>
  <key>NSAllowsArbitraryLoads</key>
  <true/>
</dict>
